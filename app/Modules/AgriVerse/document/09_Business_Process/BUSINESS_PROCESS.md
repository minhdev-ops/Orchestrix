# AgriVerse Hub: Business Process & Revenue Model

## 1. Revenue Model (Monetization)
1. **Subscription (SaaS):** Monthly fees based on the number of 3D models hosted (Basic, Pro, Advanced).
2. **3D Scan Service:** One-time fee per AI-assisted digitization job.
3. **Commission:** Transaction fee (percentage) deducted from each successful sale on the platform.

## 2. Core Business Processes
### 2.1. Onboarding & Subscription
1. Seller registers -> Chooses Subscription Plan.
2. System activates Store and sets limits based on the plan.

### 2.2. Product Digitization Flow
1. Seller uploads 360-degree video.
2. AI Scanning Service triggers -> Generates 3D model.
3. Asset Optimization Pipeline -> Compresses to .glb.
4. Product goes live with "Verified 3D" status.

### 2.3. High-Value Transaction Flow
1. Buyer explores 3D/AR -> Clicks "Buy/Connect".
2. System generates **E-Contract** and **Digital Passport** preview.
3. Both parties sign digitally.
4. Transaction completed -> Platform collects Commission.

## 3. Multi-tier Access Control
- **Admin:** Platform-wide oversight & financial reconciliation.
- **Store Owner:** Full control over store inventory and staff.
- **Store Staff:** Manage products and orders (no access to financial settings).
- **Buyer:** Browse, interact, and purchase.
