Preparing Your Sanctuary

To ensure your new specimens thrive immediately upon arrival, we recommend following these precise technical acclimation protocols.

light_mode

LIGHT ACCLIMATION

Prepare a space with 200-400 foot-candles (indirect bright light). Avoid direct South-facing exposure for the first 72 hours to prevent photosynthetic shock.

thermostat

THERMAL STABILITY

Calibrate your room temperature between 65°F and 78°F. Ensure relative humidity is at least 50% for the Monstera Albo to prevent leaf-tip browning.

water_drop

SOIL PREPARATION

Prepare a moisture-wicking substrate. We recommend a 3:1 ratio of orchid bark to perlite for optimal root aeration and scientific drainage.

monitoring

VITALITY MONITORING

Download our digital care app. Use the provided tracking number to sync your plant's specific growth profile for automated care alerts.
