# AgriVerse Hub: AI Services

## 1. AI 3D Digitization
- **Photogrammetry:** Multi-view image analysis for geometry reconstruction.
- **NeRF (Neural Radiance Fields):** High-fidelity reconstruction for complex artistic plants.
- **3D Gaussian Splatting:** Real-time rendering of complex scanned scenes.

## 2. AI Optimization & Automation
- **AI Mesh Simplification:** Intelligent reduction of polygon density at non-critical areas.
- **Auto-Rigging:** Detection of mechanical joints for automated animation of machinery.

## 3. AI User Support
- **RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation):** Chatbot trained on agricultural technical specs and machine manuals.
- **Computer Vision:** Automatic health diagnosis of plants from user photos before marketplace approval.
